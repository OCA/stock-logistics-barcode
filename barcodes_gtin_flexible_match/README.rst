This module enhances Odoo's product barcode search mechanism to
seamlessly handle differences between GTIN-13 and GTIN-14 (EAN-13 with a
leading zero) formats.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Use Cases / Context
===================

In real-world logistics and retail environments, physical barcode labels
or scanner hardware frequently introduce or omit leading zeros (e.g.,
turning a 13-digit EAN ``5412345678901`` into a 14-digit GTIN
``05412345678901``).
